# CIF Dataset Documentation

## 1. CSTI

Purpose:
Cyber Threat Intelligence entities, relationships and threat information.

Planned use:
Knowledge Graph and evidence retrieval.

Status:
Source identification pending.

---

## 2. MITRE ATT&CK

Purpose:
Threat actors, tactics, techniques, malware, tools and relationships.

Planned use:
Knowledge Graph construction and threat reasoning.

---

## 3. NVD / CVE

Purpose:
Vulnerability descriptions, severity, CWE and affected products.

Planned use:
Vulnerability context and evidence.

---

## 4. CISA KEV

Purpose:
Known exploited vulnerabilities and exploitation context.

Planned use:
Evidence enrichment and risk reasoning.

---

## 5. CICIDS2017

Purpose:
Labeled network traffic and attack observations.

Planned use:
Detection experiments and evaluation.

---
